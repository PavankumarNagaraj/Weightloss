# 🏪 Cafe Management Tabs Explained

## 📋 **Overview**

Your cafe management system now has **7 tabs**, each serving a specific purpose:

---

## 🛒 **1. Orders**
**Purpose:** Track daily customer orders

**What it does:**
- Record customer orders
- Track order items and quantities
- Calculate total amounts
- View order history
- Mark orders as completed

**Use case:** When a customer orders food/drinks

---

## 📖 **2. Menu**
**Purpose:** Manage your cafe menu items

**What it does:**
- Add/edit menu items
- Set prices
- Define ingredients and quantities
- Track which items are available
- Manage item categories

**Use case:** Adding new dishes or updating prices

---

## 📦 **3. Inventory**
**Purpose:** Track current stock levels of raw materials

**What it does:**
- View current stock of all materials
- Set minimum stock levels
- Get low stock alerts
- Add new materials
- Update stock when you receive purchases

**Use case:** Check if you have enough flour, milk, etc.

**Key difference from Purchases:**
- Inventory = **What you have NOW**
- Purchases = **What you bought** (history)

---

## 💰 **4. Purchases**
**Purpose:** Record raw material purchases from suppliers

**What it does:**
- Record what you bought
- Track supplier names
- Log purchase amounts
- View purchase history
- Automatically updates inventory

**Use case:** When you buy 10kg flour from supplier

**Example:**
```
Purchased: 10kg Flour from ABC Suppliers
Price: ₹500
Date: Dec 11, 2025
→ Inventory updated: Flour stock +10kg
```

---

## 📈 **5. Investments** ⭐ **NEW!**
**Purpose:** Track partner capital investments

**What it does:**
- Record monthly investments from partners
- Track total investment by each partner
- View monthly investment totals
- See all-time investment summary
- Add notes for each investment

**Use case:** You and your partner invest ₹25,000 each every month

**Example:**
```
Partner 1: ₹25,000 (Dec 2025)
Partner 2: ₹25,000 (Dec 2025)
Total this month: ₹50,000
```

**Features:**
- ✅ Track multiple partners
- ✅ Monthly grouping
- ✅ Total by partner
- ✅ Edit/delete records
- ✅ Add notes

---

## 📅 **6. Subscription Orders**
**Purpose:** Manage recurring customer subscriptions

**What it does:**
- Track customers with monthly subscriptions
- Record subscription payments
- View active subscriptions
- Manage subscription renewals

**Use case:** Customer pays ₹3,000/month for daily breakfast

---

## 📊 **7. Dashboard**
**Purpose:** Overview of cafe performance

**What it does:**
- Today's orders count
- Today's revenue
- Total orders (all time)
- Low stock alerts
- Recent orders
- Quick stats

**Use case:** Quick glance at how the cafe is doing

---

## 🎯 **Do You Need Both Purchases AND Inventory?**

### **YES! Here's why:**

### **Purchases Tab:**
- **Historical record** of what you bought
- Track spending over time
- Supplier management
- Cost analysis
- Tax/accounting purposes

**Example:** "How much did we spend on flour last month?"

### **Inventory Tab:**
- **Current status** of what you have
- Real-time stock levels
- Low stock alerts
- Prevent running out of materials

**Example:** "Do we have enough flour for today's orders?"

---

## 🔄 **How They Work Together:**

```
1. You buy 10kg flour → Record in PURCHASES
2. Purchases automatically updates INVENTORY
3. You use flour for orders → INVENTORY decreases
4. Inventory shows low stock → Buy more (back to step 1)
```

---

### **Monday Morning:**
