# 🥚 Adding Egg Whites to Inventory

## Quick Guide

You can now add "Egg Whites" as a separate inventory item alongside regular "Eggs" in your cafe inventory.

---

## 📋 How to Add Egg Whites

### Step 1: Go to Inventory Tab
Navigate to **Cafe Management → Inventory**

### Step 2: Click "Add Item"
Click the **"+ Add Item"** button

### Step 3: Fill in the Details

**Basic Information:**
- **Name**: `Egg Whites` or `Egg White`
- **Unit**: `ml` or `gm` (depending on how you measure)
- **Category**: `Dairy & Eggs` or `Refrigerated`
- **Inventory State**: `raw` (since egg whites are stored raw)

**Stock Information:**
- **Current Stock**: Your current quantity
- **Min Stock**: Set your reorder threshold

## 💡 Usage Tips

### 1. **Separate from Whole Eggs**
- Keep "Egg Whites" as a separate inventory item from "Eggs"
- This allows precise tracking when recipes use only whites

### 2. **Unit Conversion**
- **1 large egg white** ≈ 30-35ml ≈ 33g
- **3 egg whites** ≈ 100ml ≈ 100g

### 3. **Recipe Usage**
When creating menu items:
- For dishes using whole eggs: Use "Eggs" (1 egg = 45g)
- For dishes using only whites: Use "Egg Whites" (100ml or 100g)

### 4. **Common Recipes Using Egg Whites**
- Egg White Omelette
- Protein Pancakes
- Meringues
- Angel Food Cake
- Egg White Scramble
- Protein Shakes

## ✅ Benefits of Separate Tracking

1. **Accurate Costing**: Different prices for egg whites vs whole eggs
2. **Precise Nutrition**: Better calorie/macro calculations
3. **Inventory Control**: Track usage of each separately
4. **Menu Flexibility**: Offer both regular and high-protein options

---

## 🔄 Inventory State

For Egg Whites, set:
- **Inventory State**: `🥩 Raw (As Purchased)`

This ensures proper inventory deduction when used in cooked dishes.
